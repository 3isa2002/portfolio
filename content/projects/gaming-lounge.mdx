## Problem

A PlayStation shop with 21 screens was tracking by hand who sat at which screen, for how long, on
which console, and what each customer owed.

## Approach

An Android app on a tablet at the counter:

- **Screen dashboard:** 21 color-coded buttons, free or busy, with a running count of each.
- **Sessions:** tap a free screen, pick PS4 or PS5 and Single or Multi; a timer starts and keeps
  running even if the app is closed, with a manual correction for the start time.
- **Automatic pricing:** cost from device, mode and elapsed time, rounded to the nearest tier
  (quarter, half, three quarters, full hour); a capped manual discount.
- **History and shift reports:** every finished session is written to Firebase Firestore; reports
  filter by screen, device, mode and time range, with a one-tap "current shift" view and totals.

## Result

It runs at the counter day to day, replacing the paper log: the owner sees free screens at a glance
and closes each shift with a report of time and revenue.
