## Problem

At the Systems Branch, engineering estimations, civil and electromechanical (MEP) contracts,
consultant reviews and payments were tracked in loose spreadsheets and paper binders.

## Approach

A PHP/MySQL web application that follows each project's whole document lifecycle:

- **Civil and MEP workflows** with discipline-specific forms, from estimation and consultant review
  to contract, supply statements and final handover.
- **Financial tracking:** disbursements across installments against the contract value, with a live
  execution percentage, tax status, and estimated versus final cost.
- **Cost estimations module** for preliminary estimates, with attached scans, Excel breakdowns and
  signed PDFs.
- **Reports:** Excel and CSV exports filtered by date, engineer and project type (PhpSpreadsheet),
  and print-ready views.
- **Role-based access** for administrators and engineers, and scheduled backups (daily, weekly or
  monthly) with a retention policy.

## Result

The branch's estimations and contracts moved from paper to one searchable, backed-up system, with
each engineer seeing their own projects and deadlines.
