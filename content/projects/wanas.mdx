## Problem

I wanted a desk companion that actually talks back in Egyptian Arabic instead of the usual English
assistant voices: something with a face on a small screen, a microphone and a speaker, that answers
when spoken to.

## Approach

The robot is an ESP32-S3 with four peripherals: a GC9A01 round display on SPI, an INMP441 MEMS
microphone and a MAX98357A Class-D amplifier on two separate I2S ports, and a TTP223 touch sensor.
I calculated the 3.3 V / 5 V power budget, added capacitors to stop Wi-Fi brownout resets, and
documented the wiring map.

Its "brain" is a FastAPI backend on the local network. It takes a typed message or a recorded voice
clip, transcribes speech with the Google Web Speech API, picks a reply, and answers with the text,
a synthesized Egyptian Arabic voice (edge-tts, cached on disk so repeated replies are instant) and
an animation state for the robot's face.

The intent engine handles greetings, jokes, the time, questions about who Wanas is, and live
prayer times for your location. A small browser page tests the whole flow from a laptop when the
hardware isn't on the desk.

## Result

A working desk robot that answers in Egyptian Arabic with its own voice and face. The intent engine
is deliberately simple, keyword rules rather than a language model, so it covers the set of things
Wanas is meant to say and nothing beyond it.
