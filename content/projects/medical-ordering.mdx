## Problem

A Software Engineering course project: a working multi-role web application for ordering medical
equipment (crutches, masks, blood pressure and sugar monitors), built with Java EE.

## Approach

Customers, staff and administrators share one login and land on their own pages. Every entity has
its own DAO, 13 servlets handle the flows, and customers are emailed when a product becomes
available (an observer pattern).

My modules were the login/authentication controller and the email notification system. Later I
went back through the whole codebase to fix security issues: every SQL query parameterized,
credentials moved out of the source, the login form switched to POST, and passwords hashed with
BCrypt.

## Result

A team project where my part is visible in the code itself (the `@author` tags), plus the security
pass that made it fit to publish.
