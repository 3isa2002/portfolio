## Problem

A tourism client needed customers to browse and book trips and pay online, and staff to manage it
all, on mobile and on the web.

## Approach

I gathered the requirements with the client and owned the product end to end: a Flutter mobile
client, an admin dashboard and a public web portal in one monorepo, with booking and online payment
flows (Paymob) on a Supabase/PostgreSQL backend secured with row-level security, deployed on Vercel.
