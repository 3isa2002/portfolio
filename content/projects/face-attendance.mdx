## Problem

The face side of MAVS, my graduation project at Future University in Egypt (the ID-card side is the
[Arabic OCR pipeline](/projects/arabic-ocr)). An attendance system has to recognize a person's face,
must not be fooled by someone holding up a photo or a phone screen, and its records must not be
quietly altered later.

## Approach

It builds on two open-source projects: a face-attendance system for capture, registration and
matching, and Silent-Face-Anti-Spoofing for liveness detection. My part was what joins and secures
them:

- **One flow:** every registration and verification runs through the anti-spoofing check before
  face recognition.
- **Digital signature:** each registered user gets a signature built from their 68 facial
  landmarks (dlib) and AES encryption, so every registration is a signed, tamper-evident record.
- **Storage and UI:** a SQLite layer storing each user's image, face embedding and signature, and
  the Tkinter registration and verification screens.

## Result

Photos and screen replays are rejected before recognition runs, and each registration carries a
signature that exposes tampering. The public repository ships without any face data: the database
folder is empty on purpose, since face images and embeddings are personal data.
