## Problem

Many Egyptians chat in Franco: Arabic words written with Latin letters and digits (`3` for `ع`,
`7` for `ح`). Typing it letter by letter is slow; I wanted to just speak Arabic and get Franco.

## Approach

A real system keyboard (an Android input method), available in any app with a text field:

- Tap the mic and speak; Android's speech recognizer (Egyptian Arabic) transcribes it.
- The recognized Arabic is converted to Franco as you speak, shown as composing text and committed
  when you stop. The character mapping (`ع → 3`, `ح → 7`, `خ → 5`, `ء → 2`…) is unit-tested.
- The keyboard itself is a Kotlin `InputMethodService`; its UI is Flutter, connected through
  platform channels. A setup screen grants the microphone permission and opens the keyboard
  settings.

## Result

It works as an everyday keyboard on my phone. It isn't on the Play Store yet: the release build
still needs its own signing key.
