## Problem

My graduation project, MAVS, was a multi-modal authentication and verification system: a person's
face (see the [face attendance module](/projects/face-attendance)) and their national ID card.
The ID side needed reliable personal data from scanned Egyptian national ID cards: Arabic script,
varied scan quality, and no room for a wrong field ending up in the database.

## Approach

I built the OCR and data-extraction module end to end: image pre-processing, Arabic-script field
recognition, parsing into structured records, rule-based validation, and persistence to MySQL.

## Result

Extracted fields were tested against the source images, and failure cases such as low-quality scans
and partially readable fields were handled before anything reached storage.
