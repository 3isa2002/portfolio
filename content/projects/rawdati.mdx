## Problem

What a child ate, how they felt and what they learned at the nursery usually reaches the parents
as a paper notebook or scattered chat messages, and the teacher writes it again for every child.

## Approach

A Next.js (App Router) app on Supabase, branded "Little Blossoms", with a portal for each role
behind sign-in and route guards:

- **Teachers** take attendance, see their class and fill a daily log for each child: breakfast,
  lunch and snack (none, some or all), mood, behavior, toilet, and the subjects covered (Arabic,
  English, math, science, Montessori).
- **Parents** see the day as a timeline with a daily summary, look back through earlier days, and
  message the teacher.
- **Admins** manage children, teachers, classes and settings from a dashboard.

The daily log is validated by one Zod schema, writes go through server actions, messages arrive
live through Supabase Realtime, and the app installs as a PWA with an offline page.

A working prototype of the three portals with a demo seed script. It is not deployed at the moment.
