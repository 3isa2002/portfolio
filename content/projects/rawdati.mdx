## Problem

In most nurseries every child carries a paper notebook: the teacher writes what the child ate, how
they slept and behaved, and what they learned, and the parent reads it at home. It is slow to
write for a whole class, easy to lose, and the nursery itself has no record of it. Rawdati was
designed as a product to sell to nurseries that replaces the notebook with a system tracking
everything about each child.

## Approach

A mobile-first Next.js app on Supabase that installs on the phone like a native app (the demo
nursery is branded "Little Blossoms"). One sign-in sends each user to the portal for their role:

- **The nursery (admin)** adds children, teachers and classes from a dashboard. Adding a child
  also creates the parent's account and links it to the child.
- **The teacher** checks each child in and out, which records arrival and departure times, and
  fills the daily log: meals (breakfast, lunch and snack, each none, some or all), mood, behavior,
  toilet, nap times, and the subjects covered (Arabic, English, math, science, Montessori) with a
  comment on each. Teachers can message a parent directly or send a broadcast.
- **The parent** sees their child's day in detail as a timeline, with arrival and departure, looks
  back through earlier days, and chats with the teacher.

The daily log form is one Zod schema with React Hook Form, every write goes through a server
action, a saved log refreshes the parent's view, chat messages arrive live through Supabase
Realtime, and the app is a PWA with an offline page.

## Result

A working prototype of the three portals with a demo seed script. It has not been sold to a
nursery yet and is not deployed at the moment.
