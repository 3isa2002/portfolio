## Problem

A food-delivery service needs a storefront customers enjoy using, in Arabic and English, and an
admin side that keeps orders and drivers moving.

## Approach

A Next.js (App Router) frontend and a FastAPI backend:

- **Customers:** English (LTR) and Arabic (RTL) with next-intl, a cart and checkout, live order
  tracking with cancellation of pending orders, and JWT sign-in.
- **Admins:** a localized dashboard to move orders through Pending, Preparing, Out for delivery and
  Delivered, assign drivers inline, and watch revenue and active orders.
- **Backend:** FastAPI with SQLAlchemy, SQLite locally and PostgreSQL in production, passwords
  hashed with bcrypt.
