## Problem

Replying to dozens of WhatsApp messages a day takes time, and generic AI replies sound nothing like me.
I wanted drafts that match my tone, my slang and my usual answers — without sending my chats to a cloud API.

## Approach

Exported chats are cleaned, split into conversation turns and embedded into a local ChromaDB index.
For each incoming message, the closest past exchanges are retrieved and passed to a local model running in Ollama,
which drafts a reply in the same style.
