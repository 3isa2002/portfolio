## Problem

A practical first Flutter project: something real to build while learning Flutter and state
management.

## Approach

Pick a subject (Math, Physics, Chemistry or History), answer multiple-choice questions in Arabic,
and see your score in a result dialog. Quiz state lives in a `ChangeNotifier` with the Provider
package.

## Result

A small working app; the questions are still hard-coded, a couple per subject.
